# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision
**Tredit** is a premium, digital diary and community platform for responsible travelers. 
Our mission is to increase responsible traveling, encourage sustainable choices, and provide a clutter-free, premium space for travelers to document and share their trips.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Newcomers:** People new to traveling who want to learn how to travel responsibly.
- **Genuine Travelers:** Experienced travelers who want to contribute meaningful experiences and document their journeys.

## 3. Core Features
- **Tredit Score:** A gamified metric that rewards ethical, sustainable, and highly-rated travel experiences.
- **Active Trip Dashboard:** Real-time trip tracking with interactive maps (Leaflet) and chronological timelines.
- **Trip Journaling & Pins:** Drop pins with precise GPS coordinates or manual location entries. Add photos and notes.
- **The Ledger:** An archive of "Finished" trips and a dashboard showcasing the user's lifetime Tredit score and travel impact.
- **Explore & Destination Discovery:** Discover immersive destinations and Insider Pins from the community.
- **Social Features (Phase 4):** Follow other users, upvote "Insider Hotspots", and private sharing mechanisms.

## 4. User Personas
- **The Ethical Explorer:** Wants to minimize carbon footprint and leave positive impacts on local communities.
- **The Digital Nomad:** Wants a beautiful, reliable way to document extensive travels and share curated guides with a network.
